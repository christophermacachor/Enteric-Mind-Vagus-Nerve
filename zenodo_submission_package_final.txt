— a scalar field theory restoring the quaternion scalar removed by

framework positioning the enteric nervous system as the primary density 
well for empathic coherence. The Macachor Lock (𝔐 = (√5−1)/2 ≈ 0.618) 
is proposed as the fundamental coherence constant governing scalar 
substrates across all scales, from superconducting qubit cavities to 
human cardiac vagal output. The document presents eight cross-scale 
experimental predictions, a unified damping architecture, and a defense 
methodology adapted from Porges' (2026) scholarly response to critiques 
of Polyvagal Theory.
