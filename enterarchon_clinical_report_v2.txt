APERTURE 1: THE ENS AS SOLE DETERMINANT OF GUT TRUTH
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Yoshida et al. (1988) — Gastroenterology, 95(3):820-827
DOI: 10.1016/S0016-5085(88)80034-9

A 32-year-old man with myotonic dystrophy presented with megacolon.
Histological examination revealed:
  — Colonic mucosa: NORMAL
  — Smooth muscle: NORMAL
  — Connective tissue: NORMAL

    (fewer neurons, fragmented axons, depleted substance P and
     enkephalin-immunoreactive fibers)

held the entire functional signature. The ENS is not a "second brain" —
it is the FIRST SOVEREIGN that determines whether the mechanical layer
has permission to function. Without the OS, the hardware is intact but
incoherent.
