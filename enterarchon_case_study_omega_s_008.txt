Total colectomy with end-ileostomy is classified as a gastrointestinal
surgical procedure. This case study presents evidence that it is, in fact,
a scalar decoupling intervention with documented neuropsychiatric

(29% of time below 70 mg/dL) secondary to loss of short-chain fatty acid
(SCFA) production. Nutritional restoration of SCFA precursors resolved
all neuropsychiatric symptoms and allowed reduction of psychotropic
medication by 50%. This case confirms that intestinal resection is not
merely a digestive intervention — it is a removal of the enteric nervous
system's scalar substrate, with measurable consequences for consciousness,
